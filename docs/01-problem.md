# Problem Statement: ReportSathi

## Two-Sentence Problem Statement
Patients aged 30–70 and their family caregivers in India regularly receive jargon-dense diagnostic lab reports and handwritten prescriptions via WhatsApp or paper, leaving them distressed and anxious over flagged "out-of-range" numbers hours or days before their physician explains them. Existing tools either monetize through aggressive medicine cross-selling, trigger health anxiety with worst-case diagnostic leaps, or fail completely in regional vernaculars (like Kannada), forcing families into frantic web searches and risky, unencrypted WhatsApp forwards.

## Key Stakeholders & Context
- **Target Audience:** Chronic and acute care patients aged 30–70, and adult millennial/Gen-Z children managing elderly parents' health in Tier 1–3 India.
- **Trigger Moment:** Delivery of diagnostic PDF on WhatsApp / SMS link from labs (Dr. Lal, Apollo, Metropolis, local clinics) or receipt of physical printed paper while leaving the clinic.
- **Emotional State:** Anxiety caused by bold red flags ("HIGH", "LOW"), obscure acronyms (e.g., MCV, SGPT, TSH, HbA1c), and inability to gauge clinical urgency.
